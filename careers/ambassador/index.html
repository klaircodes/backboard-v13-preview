What the program is

A university ambassador program for students across multiple universities. Ambassadors would run workshops and other on-campus activities to help grow Backboard's presence. Participation cadence is flexible and can vary by ambassador.

What we want to assess

Whether they'd be a strong representative of Backboard, quality of work / projects, and fit for the approval process — this is not an open-admit program.

Program benefits

Hourly support

Hourly support from Backboard.

Early access

Early access to products, plus dev credits.

Personal Branding

Get paid to grow your personal brand and social media presence, while promoting Backboard's products.

Internship Pipeline

Get direct consideration for future internship opportunities at Backboard.io.

Roster consideration

Opportunity to be considered for Backboard's forward-deployed engineer roster.

Launch scope

Initial launch is focused on North America. We may keep it open globally for English-speaking applicants.
