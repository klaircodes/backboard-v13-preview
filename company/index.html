Helping teams do their best work with AI

Bringing more clarity, confidence, and momentum to the way ideas become real.

The global AI workflow is moving faster than the systems meant to support it. Teams are still stitching together prompts, context, tools, and reviews by hand.

We are building Backboard to connect those pieces into one shared system: planning the work, preserving context, and helping teams ship production-grade software with the models they already want to use.

Backboard is an AI infrastructure company that puts the power of advanced AI in the hands of the companies and developers who use it. We build infrastructure that runs under the customer’s own control, with security and privacy by design, so enterprises and developers can deploy advanced AI without giving up ownership of their data.

What we believe

Ship over talk. We measure progress by what is live, not what is discussed. Working software beats slide decks every time.

Own the outcome. Every person here has the autonomy to make real decisions and the accountability that comes with them.

Clarity over consensus. We write things down, think in public, and make decisions quickly. No death by committee.
